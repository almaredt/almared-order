# Privacy Policy — ALMARED Order

_Last updated: October 9, 2026_

ALMARED Order ("the app") is published by ALMARED TRADING L.L.C, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. The app helps you build order lists with product photos and export them as PDF or Excel files.

## Data we collect
**None.** The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no connection to any server operated by us. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal or business information.

## Data stored on your device
Orders you create — product photos, codes, quantities, prices and invoice details — are stored only inside the app on your own phone. They are deleted when you delete the order or uninstall the app.

## Camera
The app asks for camera permission only so you can photograph products for your order list. Photos are saved inside the app on your device and are never uploaded.

## Files you export or share
When you choose to save or share a PDF, Excel or backup file (for example to WhatsApp, email or your Downloads folder), the file is handed to the app or folder you pick. What happens to it next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

## Children
The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address.
